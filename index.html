FUN STATS

PRIVATE SCREEN

  1. Each round one player hosts. A round is five random cards, each an item drawn in a random colour from the grid. The host never chooses them.
  2. A pop-up names the two ends of the spectrum, like Light on the left and Heavy on the right.
  3. For each card: the host gives a clue about the item and the seekers guess it. The host gives a clue about the colour and the seekers guess it. Be creative with clues: you can’t say the card’s own item or any colour name, but comparisons like a lemon, the ocean or grass are fair game. Four words at most, and no repeating words between your two clues.
  4. Then the host places the card in one of five slots, and every seeker places the card they think the host has. A slot can only be used once per round. Changed your mind? Tap a card you already placed, then tap another tile to move it or swap places. If the host does this, everyone gets an announcement.
  5. Reveal! Cards flip one by one. Item +1, exact colour +1, exact slot +2, next-door slot +1. Up to 4 per card and 20 per round. The host scores 1 for every exact slot.
  6. Everyone hosts once, then the HUE CHAMPION is crowned (a tie is A PERFECT SPECTRUM). Nobody is out: scores just keep adding up. Playing bots? Each game mixes sharp, steady and dizzy bots, and the 150 cards keep it fresh.
